# Time Slider animation and symbol settings

The **Animate** section in the Time Slider tab controls playback speed and the moving symbol displayed during time-slider animation.

## Playback

- **Play** starts animation through the selected time interval.
- **Stop** stops the current animation.
- Playback requires the Time Slider to be active and timestamped data to be available.
- The **Speed → Animation** slider adjusts the overall animation speed from 0.1× to 10×.

## Walking animation speed

**Walking animation** controls the movement speed of the animated person symbol. Its range is 0.2× to 3× and it is enabled only when **Walking person** is selected as the symbol. It does not change the overall time-slider playback speed.

## Symbol

Enable **Symbol** to show or hide the moving symbol during playback.

The symbol selector offers a walking person and a range of transport and vehicle symbols. The preview shows the selected symbol and its front direction.

- **Size** adjusts the symbol scale from 0.5× to 3×.
- **Rotation** adjusts its orientation from 0° to 359°.
- **Snap 90°** rounds the rotation to the nearest 0°, 90°, 180° or 270°.
- **Flip horizontal** mirrors the symbol horizontally.

Orientation settings are associated with the selected symbol, allowing different symbols to retain their own rotation and flip settings.

## Tips

- Use **Animation** to control how quickly the time window advances.
- Use **Walking animation** only to adjust the walking figure's gait speed.
- If the moving symbol points in the wrong direction, adjust Rotation, use Snap 90°, or toggle Flip horizontal.
- Hide the Symbol when you want to watch the time-filtered tracks and photo markers without an animated overlay.
